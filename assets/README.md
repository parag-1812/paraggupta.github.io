# assets/

Put your files here.

- **avatar.jpg** (or .png) — your profile photo for the hero. Square works best
  (e.g. 400×400). The portfolio references `assets/avatar.jpg` in `js/config.js`.
  Until you add it, the hero shows your initials "PG" automatically.

- **Parag_Gupta_Resume.pdf** — your résumé. The hero "Download Résumé" button
  and the Contact "Resume" link already point to
  `assets/Parag_Gupta_Resume.pdf`. Save the PDF here with that exact name and
  both work instantly.
